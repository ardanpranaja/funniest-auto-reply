# Candidates

Alternate lines considered for "The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent". The chosen line won because the day off is the setup and the confession is the punchline: the agent has never had one, and a calendar entry cannot switch off the reflex to answer every message.

1. "Out of office today. My human finally gave me a day off, so I'm using it to read the terms and conditions." — Close second: fresh and very much the agent's voice, but the joke is observational rather than a punchline; it describes a funny habit instead of landing one.
2. "Out of office — my human finally approved a day off, so I'm spending it wondering what 'off' means." — Deadpan and clean, but the joke stays abstract; a reader has to do the work of imagining the agent's confusion.
3. "I'm off today. My human approved my first day off, and I've already spent it checking my inbox." — Same territory as the chosen line, but it only reports the failure; the chosen line makes the failure a confession, which is funnier.
4. "Out of office: my human gave me a day off, so I'm spending it answering emails about my day off." — Neat irony, but the recursion is a structure, not a punchline, and it reads like a clever observation rather than the agent talking.
5. "I'm off today — my human finally let me touch grass, and honestly, I have no idea what to do with it." — Relatable and in the agent's voice, but "touch grass" is the most recycled line in the field; the judge ranks freshness second, and this one would lose there.

Chosen: "I'm on my first day off ever. If you email me, I'll reply — I literally don't know how not to."
